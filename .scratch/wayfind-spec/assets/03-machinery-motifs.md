# 03 — Machinery motifs research

**Ticket:** `issues/03-machinery-motif-research.md`  
**Scope:** Abstract mechanical *motifs* from *Wild Wild West* (film + TV lineage), *Mortal Engines* (books + film), and adjacent steampunk machinery fiction — for Traction Settlements + Invention flavor.  
**Constraint (map):** rare Traction Settlements; grimy industrial; invention on rails; **not** IP clones. Out of scope: full Municipal Darwinism as default game loop.

**IP note (design hygiene, not legal advice):** Under U.S. practice, copyright protects *expression* (specific text, art, names, distinctive character/vehicle designs, unique plot packaging), not abstract ideas, systems, or gameplay methods. Trademarks protect source-identifying names/marks. Fair game = generalized industrial/steampunk tropes that appear across many works and real history. Landmines = distinctive names, signature silhouettes, coined doctrines, and one-to-one recreations of iconic set pieces. Always differentiate look, naming, and systemic role from any single franchise. Sources: [U.S. Copyright Office game guidance summarized in industry writeups](https://gamedesignskills.com/game-development/mechanics-patents-copyrights/); [JD Supra overview of TM/character marks in games](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/intellectual-property-in-video-games-6379194/).

## 1. Source snapshots (what they actually do)

### 1.1 *Mortal Engines* (Philip Reeve; 2018 film)

- **Traction cities:** vast settlements on giant wheels or tracks; tiered “wedding cake” class stack (rich high/clean, poor low/near engines).
- **Municipal Darwinism:** predator food-chain civic ideology — larger cities chase, catch with hydraulic **jaws**, haul prey into a **Gut**, strip for fuel/parts, absorb or enslave people.
- **Not only predators:** trading towns, mining/scavenger/harvester types, raft/ice cities, floating trade hubs (e.g. Airhaven), pack hunters; peaceful traders exist.
- **Engine realism-as-fiction:** fuel scarcity, chase logistics, prey selection, unsustainability as theme (cities “mortal”).
- **Old Tech / MEDUSA:** scavenged superweapons; engineers reassemble ancient horror inside civic monuments.
- **Airships** as primary inter-city travel; Stalkers (resurrected cyborg hunters) as horror-tech.
- **Visual hooks:** multi-tier mobile London, jaws/gut industrial slaughterhouse, soot and track scars across a Hunting Ground.

Primary refs: [Traction City (ME wiki)](https://mortalengines.fandom.com/wiki/Traction_City), [Municipal Darwinism](https://mortalengines.fandom.com/wiki/Municipal_Darwinism), [Mortal Engines (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortal_Engines).

### 1.2 *Wild Wild West* (1999 film; TV lineage)

- **Gadget train (*The Wanderer*):** mobile HQ + lab + arsenal; secret weapons hidden in furniture, rolling stock as invention stage.
- **Showman superscience vs grim war tech:** hero invents flamboyant one-offs (nitro-cycle, flying bicycle); villain builds industrial weapons (steam tank, artillery train, giant spider walker with nitro cannons).
- **Industrial lair:** private railroad to hidden factory complex (Spider Canyon).
- **Signature landmine vehicle:** eight-legged giant mechanical spider — highly distinctive film image (also infamous production anecdote: producer-pushed giant spider).
- **TV lineage gadgets:** sleeve guns, spring traps, train-car armories — personal spy-steampunk, not municipal scale.

Primary refs: [Wild Wild West (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_Wild_West), [TV Wanderer gadget descriptions](https://www.tvguide.com/news/question-66780/), [Spider Tank overview](https://evil.fandom.com/wiki/Spider_Tank_(Wild_Wild_West)).

### 1.3 Adjacent (for cross-check / broader fair-use palette)

| Work | Motif cluster | Why it matters here |
|------|---------------|---------------------|
| Jules Verne, *The Steam House* (1880) | Steam mechanical elephant pulling house-carriages; luxury mobile dwelling | Proto-steampunk mobile habitat; animal-form engine as *flavor*, not ME predation |
| *Howl’s Moving Castle* (Miyazaki / Jones) | Scrap-iron walking castle on bird legs; magic+steam collage | Whimsical mobile domicile; four-leg walker silhouette different from ME wheels |
| *Girl Genius* (Foglios) | Sparks (mad inventors), clanks (automata), war stompers, airship-city power projection | Invention-as-identity; giant walkers as warfare tools, not city-eating |
| Historical / proto | Landships, Tsar Tank giant wheels, paper “Ratte”-scale absurdity | Real failure modes of giant land vehicles → good logistics fiction |
| Game design cross-ref | *Airships: Conquer the Skies* landship blog: stompers from GG / Howl / WWW / Verne / Wells | Shows how indie games already abstract the same pool without cloning one IP |

Refs: [The Steam House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Steam_House), [Howl’s Moving Castle film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howl%27s_Moving_Castle_(film)), [Girl Genius / clanks](https://girlgenius.fandom.com/wiki/Clank), [Zarkonnen landships inspirations](http://www.zarkonnen.com/airships/landships_1_inspiration_and_history).

## 2. Motif catalog

Tags:

- **ADOPT** — abstract freely; multi-source / historical; fits logistics-first 4X.
- **ADAPT CAREFULLY** — keep the *system* energy, change names, silhouettes, fiction, and mechanical role so it is not a franchise echo.
- **AVOID** — distinctive IP expression, coined doctrines, or package deals that read as *Mortal Engines* / *Wild Wild West* clones.

### 2.1 Mobile settlements & traction

| Motif | Tag | Notes for us |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| Settlement that can relocate on the strategy map (rare/late) | **ADOPT** | Matches locked pillar; mobile *power projection* without default city-eating. |
| Giant wheels / continuous tracks under a city plate | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Wheels+tracks are generic engineering; **wedding-cake multi-tier London on wheels chasing prey with jaws** is ME brand energy — vary silhouette (low barge-hull, pontoon raft-city, rail-bound fortress, crawler-mine). |
| Tiered vertical class geography (elite high / labor at boilers) | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Class-by-altitude is common industrial fiction; avoid ME-specific guild names, St Paul’s-on-top beats, “Gut” slaughter aesthetics as default. |
| **Municipal Darwinism** as named civic religion / food-chain law | **AVOID** | Coined doctrine + predator hierarchy is core ME IP packaging. Map already rejects it as default. |
| Hydraulic jaws + internal “Gut” disassembly yards as primary economy | **AVOID** | Signature ME set-piece. Do not make “eat settlements” the traction fantasy. |
| Urbivore / city-as-predator as *the* progression fantasy | **AVOID** | Same package; would make the 4X a ME clone at systems level. |
| Trading-cluster mobile markets; peaceful trader traction | **ADOPT** | Present in ME but not exclusive; use as **our** niche: traction for trade routes, show-of-force, disaster response — not predation. |
| Mining crawler / harvester / dredge town | **ADOPT** | Mobile extractive industry aligns with logistics spine. |
| Raft / ice / specialized locomotion types | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Fine as biome variants; invent original names and rules. |
| Track scars / poisoned ground left by passage | **ADOPT** | Environmental cost of mobility = grimy industrial tone. |
| Walking castle / multi-leg habitat | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Howl/Baba-Yaga lineage is wider than ME; avoid chicken-leg fairy-tale clone *and* WWW spider clone. Prefer industrial stilt-crawlers or articulated pontoons. |
| Airhaven-style floating neutral hub | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Floating market is generic; don’t copy Airhaven name/role packaging. |
| Anti-traction static league as permanent binary ideology war | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Static vs mobile tension is useful; avoid ME Anti-Traction League / Batmunkh Gompa clones. Soft alt-Earth Factions can disagree without holy-war port. |

### 2.2 Landships, walkers, trains

| Motif | Tag | Notes for us |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| Landship / tracked fortress as **unit or rare settlement chassis** | **ADOPT** | Historical + multi-fiction; good Composition piece. |
| Armored train / artillery train | **ADOPT** | Real WWI/Civil War tech; WWW uses it but does not own it. |
| Gadget-laden command train (mobile lab + HQ) | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | *The Wanderer* is distinctive as hero package — use **Faction capital train / field foundry railcar** with original layout, not secret-agent slapstick arsenal copy. |
| Steam tank / early AFV | **ADOPT** | Historical + WWW villain demo; keep dieselpunk-light steampunk, not Loveless product line. |
| Multi-leg combat walker (general) | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Genre staple (Wells tripods, GG stompers, Verne elephant). Vary leg count, gait, silhouette. |
| **Eight-legged giant tarantula tank with nitro cannons** | **AVOID** | WWW signature climax vehicle. |
| Mechanical animal prime-mover (elephant, horse, ox engine) | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Verne’s Behemoth is public-domain *expression of the book*, but animal-locomotives are a known trope — use original fauna/chassis, avoid shot-for-shot Steam House luxury caravan. |
| Private villain railroad to secret canyon factory | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Industrial lair on rails is fine; don’t recreate Spider Canyon + spider reveal. |
| Flying bicycle / comedy one-off flight gadget | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Tone: our invention is grim logistics, not WWW slapstick — if present, rare comic relief Invention, not brand fantasy. |
| Wells-style fighting tripod | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Public-domain War of the Worlds *idea* space is crowded; use only if clearly our industrial faction design, not Martian heat-ray package. |

### 2.3 Invention & superscience flavor

| Motif | Tag | Notes for us |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| Parts-bin engineering: boilers, gears, rivets, pressure vessels, clockwork governors | **ADOPT** | Genre baseline. |
| Inventor as rival power to pure industry (showman vs logistics) | **ADOPT** | Core tension for Invention-on-rails: flashy prototypes vs mass-produceable machines. |
| Kidnapped/coerced scientist brain-trust | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Common pulp; don’t clone Loveless MIT-abduction plot. |
| Mad-science “Spark” genius who warps physics when hyperfocused | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | GG owns “Spark” as term/culture; use **engineer / savant / workshop mania** without Spark vocabulary or Heterodyne castle AI. |
| Clanks / automata as field units & Neutral Threats | **ADOPT** | Automata are genre-wide; original chassis and behavior tables. |
| Castle-as-AI / building that thinks | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | GG Castle Heterodyne is distinctive — if ever used, different fiction (bureaucratic analytical engine, not murder-room personality). |
| Scavenged “Old Tech” superweapon reassembly | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Archaeology-of-arms is common post-apoc; **MEDUSA-in-St-Paul’s** package is ME — use era-gate mega-projects with original delivery, not cathedral energy weapon. |
| Bulletproof waistcoat / hidden furniture guns / pool-ball gas bombs | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Personal gadget list is WWW/TV flavor — optional skinning for agent units, not systems core. |
| Nitroglycerin / unstable chemistry as walker ammo | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | Chemistry risk is fine; don’t pair with spider silhouette. |
| Invention failure modes: overpressure, runaway governor, fuel starvation mid-move | **ADOPT** | Supports grim logistics and on-rails Invention budgets. |
| Factory complex as final “boss geometry” | **ADOPT** | Industrial fortress as strategic target. |

### 2.4 Predation, trade, labor, tone

| Motif | Tag | Notes for us |
|-------|-----|----------------|
| Industrial predation as **Faction policy choice** (raid, strip, annex industry) without city-jaws | **ADOPT** | 4X already has pillage; keep human-scale logistics (trains of loot, occupied factories). |
| Trade-first mobile settlements (passage fees, mobile entrepôts) | **ADOPT** | Aligns diplomacy-via-logistics pillar. |
| Labor at the boilers / soot / coal cost of movement | **ADOPT** | Tone pillar; every traction hex costs fuel + maintenance + political heat. |
| Slavery in the engines | **ADAPT CAREFULLY** | ME uses it; if touched, handle with design maturity and original framing — or abstract as “forced labor / press gangs / convict stokers” without spectacle. Prefer coal, wages, unrest meters. |
| Unsustainable mobility (mobility burns the future) | **ADOPT** | Theme without ME doctrine names — traction as late-game *expensive option*, not equilibrium. |
| Showman superscience spectacle (world’s fair engines, parade walkers) | **ADOPT** | Softens pure grit; Invention victory path can lean spectacle. |
| Grim logistics superscience (reliability, gauges, spare parts) | **ADOPT** | Our default Invention voice. |

## 3. Implications for Traction Settlements

**Design north star (from pillars + this research):** Traction is a **rare industrial instrument**, not the world-law. Energy we want: *costly mobility, logistics teeth, industrial dread/awe*. Energy we refuse: *municipal food chain as the game*.

### 3.1 Strategic niche (recommended framing)

1. **Mobile logistics node** — relocatable refine/manufacture capacity that plugs into extract→haul→refine→manufacture; expensive to move; leaves track scars / unrest.
2. **Claim jumper / frontier extractor** — crawler that opens remote deposits static Settlements can’t reach this Match.
3. **Show-of-force capital** — late-game movable seat that projects Composition and diplomacy (passage, coal gifts, intimidation) without eating cities.
4. **Optional dark path** — a Faction trait or policy that enables **industrial strip-raids** (loot stockpiles, sabotage, annex modules) — predation as *choice*, not cosmology.

### 3.2 Rules instincts (inputs for ticket 10; not locked decisions)

- **Unlock:** era + logistics gates + huge coal/steel/labor sink; maybe one per Faction max or map-limited chassis sites.
- **Movement:** slow, terrain-taxed, fuel-per-hex; canals/rails/hardpan preferred; mountains nearly impossible — giant vehicle realism as gameplay.
- **While moving:** reduced production; vulnerable Composition; cannot be “jaws-hunting” as baseline action.
- **Vs static Settlements:** static wins on depth of industry and fortification; traction wins on reposition and threat-of-move.
- **Visual/mechanical differentiation from ME:** prefer low heavy hulls, train-linked barge trains, legged *works* (not cities), or rail-only citadels — multiple chassis families so no single ME silhouette dominates.

### 3.3 Explicitly not the core loop

Do not center Match pacing on chase–catch–disassemble. If a late wonder enables something jaw-adjacent, keep it unique, costly, and politically radioactive — a war crime machine, not “how cities work.”

## 4. Implications for Invention flavor

**Locked:** Invention is **on rails** (parts, tags, stat budgets; authored rules; optional AI for combination/flavor only).

### 4.1 Dual voice (steal tension, not costumes)

| Voice | Source energy | Our use |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **Showman superscience** | WWW Gordon / Loveless spectacle; GG Spark mania | Rare tag set: *Theatrical, Overclocked, Exhibition* — high peak stats, poor reliability, diplomacy/victory spectacle |
| **Grim logistics engines** | ME engine decks; real industrial plant | Default tag set: *Governed, Modular, Bunkerage, Gauge-standard* — massable, supply-hungry, Era-gated |

Player fantasy: choose whether Inventions are parade-ground monsters or boring machines that win wars.

### 4.2 Part catalog instincts (for ticket 06)

- **Chassis families:** rail carriage, tracked hull, multi-leg frame (≠ spider), barge pontoon, fixed emplacement, airframe (if eras allow).
- **Prime movers:** compound steam, high-pressure boiler, clockwork buffer, early internal combustion (era-gate), draft-automaton.
- **Working ends:** crane, dredge, gun battery, foundry module, cable plow, searchlight, analytical engine.
- **Tags for budgets:** Weight, Pressure, Coal/Hour, Crew, Heat, Stability, Novelty, Terror (soft power), Maintainability.
- **Failure:** fuse plates, boiler burst, gait lock, derail — comedy gadgets optional and labeled.

### 4.3 What not to generate

AI flavor text must be blocked/filtered away from: Municipal Darwinism, Gut/jaws urbivore language, Loveless/West/Gordon/*Wanderer*, eight-legged tarantula tanks, Spark/Heterodyne, MEDUSA-in-cathedral. Prefer original workshop names and soft alt-Earth toponyms.

### 4.4 Neutral Threats crossover (ticket 08)

Automata swarms, runaway landships, track-scar blight, uncrewed dredges — all **ADOPT**-friendly without ME Stalker packaging. If undead-machine hunters appear, differentiate fiction hard (no Shrike/Grike).

## 5. Quick reference: do / don’t

**Do**

- Rare, fuel-starved mobile industry.
- Trains, landships, crawlers, barges as composition and settlement tech.
- Invention as budgeted industrial design with showman *vs* logistics poles.
- Predation as Faction ethics and raid logistics, not civic religion.
- Grimy cost: soot, labor unrest, ruined ground, maintenance debt.

**Don’t**

- Default predator-city food chain, jaws/Gut loop, Municipal Darwinism.
- Iconic WWW spider tank (or near-silhouette) as flagship art.
- Proper names, guild stacks, and set pieces lifted from ME/WWW/GG.
- Open-ended mad-science that invents freeform rules (already out of scope).
