## Question

What lessons from existing games should constrain our spec (do / don’t steal clumsily)?

Survey briefly (mechanics lessons, not reviews):
- Civ-like 4X (era structure, victory paths, SP match arc)
- Logistics / production-forward strategy (e.g. games where routes and throughput matter)
- Composition-focused strat-map combat (no separate battle layer)
- Light factory or machine-crafting inside broader strategy
- Anything adjacent to mobile bases/settlements without full Mortal Engines clone

Resolve with: a short brief of patterns to adopt, patterns to avoid, and open risks for our pillars. Link any notes as assets under `.scratch/wayfind-spec/assets/`.

## Answer

Comparable survey mapped to pillars: **Adopt** Anno-shaped shallow chains + Shadow-style dual-use logistics + Old World strat-map Composition + Shadow/HighFleet on-rails model design + Civ era readability with hard logistics gates + Offworld-style deniable industrial/economic finish lines; **Avoid** early-commit themed-only victories, battle-layer/auto-resolve gaps, Factorio depth, traction-as-default, freeform LLM Invention, unit carpets; **Risks** chain depth vs 4–8h, AI logistics, thin combat without tactics map, invention sandbox drift, un-interactive industry wins.
